DJ Kids turns your Hercules DJControl Inpulse 200 MK3 into a scratch learning machine.

LEARN REAL SCRATCH TECHNIQUES
Progressive curriculum built around the actual movements DJs use:
• Baby scratch — the foundation
• Forward, chirp, tear, scribble — intermediate skills
• Transformer, flare, orbit — advanced techniques

HOW IT WORKS
Connect your MK3 via USB. DJ Kids auto-detects it and maps every knob, fader, and jog wheel to real DJ functions. Start a training session: a breakbeat loops, a rhythm grid shows you exactly when to scratch, and the app scores your timing precision in real time.

REAL DJ HARDWARE, REAL SKILLS
This is not a tap game. You use the actual jog wheel to scratch, the crossfader to cut, and the EQ knobs to shape your sound. Every movement you learn here transfers directly to a real DJ booth.

TWO DECKS, FULL MIXER
Outside training mode you get a complete two-deck setup with:
• 3-band EQ per deck
• Pitch/tempo control
• Crossfader
• MIDI monitor to verify your mappings

COMPATIBLE WITH
• Hercules DJControl Inpulse 200 MK3 (USB class-compliant, no driver needed)

PRIVACY
DJ Kids works entirely offline. No accounts, no data collection, no internet required.
