🎧 DJ Kids

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short

DJ Kids does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. DJ Kids is a fully offline application. It does not require an internet connection to function, does not create an account, and does not send any information to external servers.

Permissions

  • USB Host — to connect a MIDI DJ controller (Hercules DJControl Inpulse 200 MK3 or compatible) via USB OTG. No data is transmitted.
  • Audio — to play scratch sound samples stored locally in the app. No microphone access is requested.

Third-party services

DJ Kids does not integrate any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social SDK.

Children

DJ Kids is designed for children. Because no data is collected, there are no special requirements beyond what is stated above.
